The Dub Report with Andrew Hairston

October 2026 · About 27 minutes

One Piece, Start to Now

One show for the whole episode. Who made One Piece, and the answer to a question most fans get wrong: who was the very first voice of Luffy. Where the manga and the anime stand this week, including the new 26-episodes-a-year format and the Elbaph English dub premiere on Crunchyroll. The Lineup names all ten English voice actors behind the Straw Hat crew with two credits each. Then the Netflix live action: how Season 2 did, and everything known about Season 3, The Battle of Alabasta, before its 2027 release.

Bible break: Psalm 139:14. Sponsor: the VIZ One Piece box sets and a few collector picks, with links on the Gear page.
