Watch the show

Tony was born in Martinsville, served twenty years in the Marine Corps, and came back home. The show covers how this town got here, what's happening now, where to eat, and what's on this weekend. Every episode ends with a credit roll naming the source for everything reported.

How the show works

  • Facts come from the record first: the City of Martinsville, Henry County, and named local outlets.
  • We say who said it. "According to" is a sentence we use a lot.
  • We encourage you to vote. We never tell you who to vote for.
  • Private people stay private.
  • If we got something wrong, reply to any Back Home Daily email and we'll fix it on the record.
