Podcast / Mission

Why we make these shows

One host. One subject. One listener who matters.

Iron Cow Productions makes podcasts the way a good teacher runs a class. The host knows the subject. The host respects the person listening. And nobody leaves without one thing they can use today.

We don't chase trends. We don't talk down. We don't pad a show to hit a number. If an episode runs long, it's because there was more worth saying, not because we needed the minutes.

The promise behind every show

You are one of one. Nobody else on this planet has your perspective, and that includes the comment you leave, good or bad. We read every one of them. Not a sample. Every one. That's not a courtesy. It's the whole point. A show made for one listener at a time has to listen back.

The shows

The Dub Report with Andrew Hairston covers anime and manga for people who watch with the English dub on. Andrew reports the new dub drops each week and names the voice actors behind the characters, every time, because the people in the booth earn the credit. Each episode has a Bible break in the middle and ends with a word for anyone who is having a hard time.

Best Pickup Lines with Jessica is about how men and women actually talk to each other, told from a woman's side of the table. Jessica takes a pickup line apart and asks what it really says. No lecture. Just the truth about what landed and what didn't.

What ties them together

Knowledge is your currency. Discipline is how you spend it. Freedom is what it buys. These shows are where we hand some of that currency over, one episode at a time, to one listener at a time.
