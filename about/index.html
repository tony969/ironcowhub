About

I'm Anthony L. Hairston. Most people call me Tony. I was born in Martinsville, Virginia, and I came back home to build this.

I served 20 years in the United States Marine Corps. I started as a military policeman and deployed for Desert Shield and Desert Storm. Later I was handed a computer job nobody expected me to finish. I finished it, and I ended up training the whole unit. After the Corps, I worked for HP.

Iron Cow Productions is what I'm building now. It is a catalog of books, shows, stories, and games that I can hand down to my children. My company, OpenClaw AI Solutions LLC, handles the video production, app building, and technology work behind it.

Everything here comes back to one belief. Every person is one of one. There will never be another you. Knowledge is your currency. Discipline is how you spend it. Freedom is what it buys.
